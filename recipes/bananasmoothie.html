Banana Smoothie

Home

Description

Enjoy this fast and easy banana smoothie. With a handful of ingredients and time, you'll be able to enjoy a sweet and healthy snack!

Ingredients

  • 2 Bananas
  • A cup of milk

Steps

  1. Peel your 2 bananas
  2. Put bananas and cup of milk in blender
  3. Blend until changes to smoothie-like texture and enjoy!
