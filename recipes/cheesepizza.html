Cheese Pizza

Home

Description

Quick, fast and easy cheese pizza recipe. With minimal time and ingredients, you'll be able to enjoy a quality meal.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Fold out dough into a round, circle shape
  2. Spread tomato sauce across the circular pizza dough
  3. Apply the mozzarela cheese across the pizza dough
  4. Bake in oven and enjoy when ready
